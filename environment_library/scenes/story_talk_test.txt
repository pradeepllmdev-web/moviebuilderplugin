In the council hall the elders argue about the quest.
Elder (angry): "No one has ever returned from the ice cave!"
Leo (serious): "Then no one has ever tried with a friend beside them."
Mara (warm): "I will go with him. Someone has to keep him out of trouble."
Elder (sad): "Then take the old map, and come back to us."

In the ice cave Leo leaps between drifting ice floes while stalactites crash around him.
