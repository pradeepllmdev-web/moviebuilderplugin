At dawn a prison train thunders across the plains while Leo escapes over the roofs and leaps from the bridge into the river.

At night a stolen dinosaur egg lies in a prehistoric valley beside a glowing volcano and Leo carries it back to its mother.

At dusk Leo climbs a sheer cliff to rescue a lost cub and swings across the gap on a rope.

In a storm Leo fights a sea serpent on the mast of a sailing ship.

An army besieges the citadel with catapults and Leo opens the gate at dawn.

At night in the gladiator arena Leo faces the rival lion in a duel before the roaring crowd.
