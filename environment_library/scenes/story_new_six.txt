At night the lion council sits around the round table to debate how to stop the coming flood.

At dawn in his workshop Leo tinkers with clockwork gears and assembles a music box with a crank.

At midnight Leo sneaks into the museum past the laser gate and the sentries to steal the jewel before the alarm sounds.

At noon Leo paddles a raft through the rapids between the boulders of the canyon river.

At dusk a wall of water floods the valley and Leo rescues a child and carries it to the hill.

At sunset Leo rides a dragon through the canyon between the rock spires and under the stone arch.
