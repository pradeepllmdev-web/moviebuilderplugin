Around the campfire Leo and Mara plan the quest for the moon crystal.
Mara (serious): "The old map says the crystal sleeps beneath the observatory dome."
Leo (warm): "Then at moonrise we wake it."
Mara (concern): "If the Rival gets there first, the valley will never see the sun again."
Leo (serious): "He will not get there first."

At night in the old observatory Leo turns the mirror wheels until a moonbeam strikes the crystal and the dome opens.

A thief snatches the star map and Leo chases him through the crowded night market, vaulting the crates he topples.

Around the campfire Mara bandages Leo's hand while Tarn the scout brings news.
Tarn (concern): "The thief took the road through the swamp. The Rival's men are with him."
Leo (angry): "Then we follow him through the swamp and we do not stop."
Mara (warm): "You will not be going alone."

Leo poles a punt through the mangrove swamp while crocodiles lunge from the dark water.

Deep in the jungle Leo swings from vine to vine across a ravine toward the ancient temple.

Inside the desert temple a giant boulder is released and Leo outruns it, jumps the pit and slides under the closing door.

Around the campfire the travellers rest after the temple and the cold wind turns to snow.
Mara (sad): "I thought we had lost you in the temple."
Leo (warm): "You will not lose me that easily."
Tarn (joy): "Look, the pass to the ice cave is open. We can reach the north by morning!"

In the ice cave Leo leaps between drifting ice floes while stalactites crash around him.

At the volcano forge Leo hammers a glowing sword on the anvil beside the lava river and quenches it.

Leo dives into the coral reef, opens a giant clam for the pearl and escapes a shark.

Leo sprints across the rotten rope bridge as the planks fall away behind him.

Leo crosses the floating city, riding the moving platforms above the clouds.

In the council hall the elder gives Leo the last key before the final climb.
Elder (serious): "The Rival has woken the stone giant, and only the core in its chest can stop it."
Leo (serious): "Then I will take the core from its chest."
Elder (concern): "Be careful. Nobody has climbed it and come down."

A stone giant lifts its arm and Leo rides the palm, walks to the shoulder and pulls the glowing core from its chest.

At sunset Leo rides a dragon through the canyon between the rock spires and under the stone arch.

On the moonlit tower the rival mage and Leo trade spells in a magic duel, and Leo's shield turns the last spell back.

Around the campfire the travellers laugh together at last.
Mara (joy): "You did it! The valley will see the sun again!"
Leo (warm): "We did it. All of us."
Tarn (joy): "Then let us go home!"
