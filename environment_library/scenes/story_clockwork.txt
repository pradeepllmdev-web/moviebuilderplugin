A thief snatches the star map and Leo chases him through the crowded night market, vaulting the crates he topples.

At night in the busy market Leo and Mara plan the heist of the star crown.
Mara (serious): "The crown sits in the clockwork tower behind three locked gates."
Leo (warm): "Then we open them one by one."
Mara (concern): "The guards change at midnight. We have one chance."

In his workshop Leo tinkers with clockwork gears and turns the crank of a music box that points the way.

At midnight Leo sneaks into the museum past the laser gate and the sentries and steals the jewel before the alarm sounds.

A prison train thunders across the plains while Leo escapes over the roofs and leaps from the bridge into the river.

Leo rides a horse galloping across the plains and jumps the fence.

Around the campfire Mara counts the clockwork keys while Leo watches the road.
Mara (serious): "Two keys are copied and one is missing."
Leo (concern): "The missing key is in the tower itself."
Mara (joy): "Then we steal it from under their noses!"

At dawn an army besieges the citadel with catapults and Leo opens the gate.

At night in the old observatory Leo turns the mirror wheels until a moonbeam strikes the crystal and the dome opens.

At the volcano forge Leo hammers a glowing sword on the anvil beside the lava river and quenches it.

Inside the desert temple a giant boulder is released and Leo outruns it, jumps the pit and slides under the closing door.

Leo sprints across the rotten rope bridge as the planks fall away behind him.

At the old council table Leo and Mara study the plans of the tower again.
Leo (serious): "The back stair is our way in and out."
Mara (angry): "And the Rival will be waiting at the top."
Leo (warm): "Then we will give him a surprise."

Deep in the jungle Leo swings from vine to vine across a ravine toward the ancient temple.

Leo dives into the coral reef, opens a giant clam for the pearl and escapes a shark.

Leo poles a punt through the mangrove swamp while crocodiles lunge from the dark water.

At dusk an avalanche thunders down the snowy mountain and Leo outruns it with the cub.

At the foot of the mountains Leo climbs a sheer cliff to rescue a lost cub and swings across the gap on a rope.

Downstream Leo paddles a raft through the rapids between the boulders of the canyon river.

Leo crosses the floating city, riding the moving platforms above the clouds.

At sunset Leo rides a dragon through the canyon between the rock spires and under the stone arch.

On the moonlit tower the rival mage and Leo trade spells in a magic duel, and Leo's shield turns the last spell back.

Around the campfire after the heist Leo and Mara look at the crown in the firelight.
Mara (joy): "We did it. We really did it!"
Leo (warm): "Together we always do."
Mara (sad): "Now we have to give it back to the valley."
