At night the lion council sits around the round table and debates who will take the quest, and Leo volunteers.

Around the campfire Leo and Pip plan the long road to the valley of the sun.
Pip (concern): "If the egg gets cold before we reach the valley it will never hatch."
Leo (serious): "Then we do not rest until it is warm."
Pip (warm): "You carry the egg and I will watch the road."

In his workshop Leo tinkers with clockwork gears and turns the crank of a music box that points the way.

Downstream Leo paddles a raft through the rapids between the boulders of the canyon river.

Deep in the jungle Leo swings from vine to vine across a ravine toward the ancient temple.

Inside the desert temple a giant boulder is released and Leo outruns it, jumps the pit and slides under the closing door.

Around the campfire Pip warms the egg while Leo sharpens the quest map.
Pip (sad): "I heard the egg thieves behind us on the river road."
Leo (angry): "Then they will meet me at the bridge."
Pip (concern): "Be careful, Leo. They are many."

Leo poles a punt through the mangrove swamp while crocodiles lunge from the dark water.

At night a stolen dinosaur egg lies in a prehistoric valley beside a glowing volcano and Leo carries it back to its mother.

At noon a herd of wild horses gallops along the river toward the mountains.

Leo rides a horse galloping across the plains and jumps the fence.

At dusk an avalanche thunders down the snowy mountain and Leo outruns it with the cub.

At the foot of the mountains Leo climbs a sheer cliff to rescue a lost cub and swings across the gap on a rope.

Around the campfire the travellers rest after the mountain and the egg begins to glow.
Leo (joy): "Look, the egg is glowing. It knows we are close!"
Pip (joy): "Then we must reach the valley before sunrise."
Leo (warm): "We will, my friend."

A wall of water floods the valley below and Leo rescues a child and carries it to the hill.

Leo sprints across the rotten rope bridge as the planks fall away behind him.

In a storm Leo fights a sea serpent on the mast of a sailing ship.

In the ice cave Leo leaps between drifting ice floes while stalactites crash around him.

At sunset Leo rides a dragon through the canyon between the rock spires and under the stone arch.

Leo crosses the floating city, riding the moving platforms above the clouds.

A stone giant lifts its arm and Leo rides the palm, walks to the shoulder and pulls the glowing core from its chest.

At noon in the gladiator arena Leo faces the rival lion in a duel before the roaring crowd.

Around the campfire at dawn Leo and Pip watch the egg crack open.
Pip (joy): "It is hatching, Leo. It is really hatching!"
Leo (warm): "Welcome to the world, little one."
Pip (warm): "You brought it home."
